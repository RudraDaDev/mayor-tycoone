# Part 1 — Vision, Core Philosophy & Core Gameplay Loop

## Vision

Project Crown is a living world simulation where the player manages a single settlement while hundreds of AI-controlled settlements evolve independently.

Instead of constructing visible buildings in a traditional city-builder, the player manages the city through statistics, upgrades, policies, contracts, investments, infrastructure, economics and strategic decisions.

Every world is procedurally generated. Every company is unique. Every city develops its own identity. Every notable person creates history. No two playthroughs should ever be identical. The game should feel alive, unpredictable, and constantly evolving.

## Core Philosophy

> The world never waits for the player.

While the player is deciding what to do:
- Companies are expanding.
- Cities are growing.
- Villages are disappearing.
- Famous people are becoming richer.
- Housing prices are changing.
- Resources are fluctuating.
- Governments are issuing contracts.
- Celebrities are travelling.
- Investors are searching for opportunities.

The player exists inside the simulation—not above it.

## Gameplay Overview

The player becomes the mayor of a small settlement. Initially, this settlement may only contain a few hundred citizens. Through careful management, it can become:
- a farming village
- an industrial town
- a financial centre
- a technology hub
- a tourist paradise
- a world-famous port
- an education capital
- or anything in between.

```text
        ▼

        │
        ▼

        │
        ▼
Infrastructure Improves
        │

        │
        ▼

```

## Game Speed & Time System

- **Speeds:** Pause, 0.5×, 1×, 2×, 4×, 8×, 16×, 32×, 64×
- **Calendar:** 365-day years, Months, Weeks, Days, Hours, Seasons (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter), Dynamic Weather.
- **Currency:** Official symbol `◈` (e.g., `◈ 50`, `◈ 18.2K`, `◈ 950K`, `◈ 18.5M`, `◈ 4.3B`).

# Part 2 — World Generation, Geography, Climate & World Economy

- **World Sizes:** Tiny, Small, Medium, Large, Huge, Custom
- **Continents & Countries:** Industrial, Agricultural, Urbanized, Tourism, Mountainous, Island Chain continents; procedural countries with GDP, Stability, Education, Technology, Infrastructure, Tourism, Trade & Tax Policies.
- **Geography & Terrain:** Ocean, River, Lake, Pond, Landlocked, Mountains, Forest, Plains, Desert, Tundra, Island, Coastline.
- **Economic Cycles:** Boom, Stable, Slowdown, Recession, Recovery; dynamic supply/demand pricing, transport costs (Road, Rail, River, Sea, Air), automatic imports/exports.

# Part 3 — City Management, Infrastructure, Services & Housing

- **Population Groups:** Children, Teenagers, Young Adults, Adults, Senior Citizens.
- **Housing & Districts:** Automatic housing development based on demand, interest rates, land value, and district identities (Downtown, Financial District, Old Town, Industrial Zone, University District, Technology Park, Luxury Waterfront, Harbour District, Suburbs, etc.).
- **Infrastructure (Levels 1–10):** Roads, Water, Electricity (Coal, Oil, Gas, Hydro, Solar, Wind, Nuclear, Fusion), Internet, Healthcare, Education, Police, Fire Department, Garbage Collection, Sewage, Parks, Public Transport (Bus, Metro, Railway, High-Speed Rail, Ferry, Ports, Airports), Monuments, Warehouses.
